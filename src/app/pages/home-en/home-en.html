The problem

One platform instead of five apps, three chats and a stack of paper.

Most schools run on a patchwork that nobody chose on purpose. Messages get lost, families feel left out, and staff spend their evenings answering the same question twice.

Chat groups with no one in charge

Important notices sink under stickers and side conversations. Teachers end up answering at 10 pm on their personal number.

Paper slips and spreadsheets

Permission slips that never come back. Fees, uniform orders and book lists tracked by hand in files only one person understands.

A different login for every task

Grades live in one system, payments in another, announcements in email. Families give up, and the school's voice gets scattered.

For teachers

Teach more. Chase less.

Your groups, courses and schedule are ready when you sign in. The routine takes minutes, so the day can go to the classroom.

For students

A safe space to learn, together.

Students get one place to see what's due, follow their progress and work with classmates, always in a space their school runs.

  • Know what's next. Assignments, due dates and the week's schedule on one screen.
  • See progress, not just a final grade. Track each course through the period.

  • Made for their age. Preschoolers don't need an account; their families follow along for them.

Safe by design

Every conversation has a place, and an adult in the room.

Skooltrak isn't an open chat app with a school logo on it. The rules that keep children safe are built into how it works, not left to a settings page.

Channels with a scope

Student conversations belong to a level, group, course or assignment. Nothing happens off to the side.

Teachers start and supervise

Administrators and teachers open student channels and can see everything said in them.

No private messages with students

Direct messages are only between the school and parents or guardians. Students never get a private line.

The school decides who's in

Access comes from your roster and roles. When a family or teacher leaves, their access leaves with them.

The platform
